## Present Simple Tense

The present simple tense is used to describe habitual actions, general truths, and permanent situations.

### Form

**Subject + Base Verb (+ s/es for third person)**

```
I/You/We/They play
He/She/It plays
```

### Examples

✅ **I play football every Saturday.**
✅ **She works as a teacher.**
✅ **Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.**
✅ **Dogs bark when they see strangers.**

### When to Use

1. **Habitual actions** - Things you do regularly
2. **General truths** - Facts that are always true
3. **Permanent situations** - States that don't change
4. **Scheduled events** - Fixed plans

### Practice

Fill in the blanks:
1. She _____ (go) to the gym three times a week.
2. Birds _____ (fly) south in winter.
3. I _____ (like) chocolate ice cream.

**Answers:** 1. goes, 2. fly, 3. like
