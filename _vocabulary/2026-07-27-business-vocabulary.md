## Business English Vocabulary

Learn key terms used in professional settings.

### Common Business Words

| Word | Definition | Example |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Deadline** | Final date to complete something | The project deadline is Friday. |
| **Meeting** | Gathering of people to discuss | We have a meeting at 10 AM. |
| **Budget** | Planned amount of money | We need to increase our budget. |
| **Profit** | Money earned after expenses | Our profit increased this quarter. |
| **Client** | Customer who buys services | The client is very satisfied. |
| **Invoice** | Bill for products/services | Send the invoice to the client. |
| **Proposal** | Suggestion or plan | I'll prepare a proposal. |
| **Contract** | Formal agreement | Sign the contract here. |

### Useful Phrases

- **"Let's touch base"** - Let's have a quick meeting
- **"On the same page"** - We agree
- **"Ballpark figure"** - Approximate amount
- **"At the end of the day"** - In conclusion
- **"Low-hanging fruit"** - Easy tasks to complete first

### Practice

Use these words in sentences about your work or studies!
