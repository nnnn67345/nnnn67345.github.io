## Best English Learning Apps

Here are the top apps to boost your English skills.

### 1. **Duolingo** 📱
- Fun, gamified learning
- 5-minute daily lessons
- Free version available

### 2. **Grammarly** ✏️
- Grammar and spelling checker
- Writing suggestions
- Browser extension available

### 3. **Speaky** 🎤
- Connect with native speakers
- Practice speaking and writing
- Video chat lessons

### 4. **BBC Learning English** 📺
- High-quality video lessons
- Authentic English content
- Free resources

### 5. **Forvo** 🔊
- Pronunciation guide
- Hear native speakers
- Thousands of words covered

### 6. **Babbel** 🌍
- Structured courses
- Interactive lessons
- Certificate available

### 7. **Memrise** 🧠
- Vocabulary learning
- Spaced repetition technique
- Community-created content

### 8. **TED-Ed** 🎓
- Educational videos
- English subtitles available
- Interactive quizzes

### 9. **QuillBot** 📝
- Paraphrasing tool
- Grammar checking
- Writing improvement

### 10. **English Attack** 🎬
- TV series clips
- Learn from real content
- Interactive lessons

### Recommendation

Start with **Duolingo** for daily practice, then add **Grammarly** for writing and **Forvo** for pronunciation!
