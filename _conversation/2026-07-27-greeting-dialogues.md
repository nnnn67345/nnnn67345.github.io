## Common Greeting Dialogues

Practice these everyday conversations.

### Dialogue 1: Formal Introduction

**Person A:** Good morning. My name is Sarah. What's your name?
**Person B:** Nice to meet you, Sarah. I'm John.
**Person A:** It's a pleasure to meet you too.

### Dialogue 2: Casual Greeting

**Person A:** Hey! How are you doing?
**Person B:** I'm doing great, thanks! How about you?
**Person A:** Can't complain! What have you been up to?
**Person B:** Just working on a project. Nothing too exciting.

### Dialogue 3: Meeting Someone at an Event

**Person A:** Hi! I'm Lisa. I'm a friend of Tom's.
**Person B:** Oh, nice to meet you! I'm Mike. How do you know Tom?
**Person A:** We went to university together.
**Person B:** That's cool! Have you two been in touch?
**Person A:** Yeah, we meet up occasionally.

### Useful Phrases

- "How do you do?" - Formal greeting
- "What's up?" - Casual greeting
- "Long time no see!" - When greeting someone you haven't seen in a while
- "It's been a while!" - Similar to above
- "Nice to meet you!" - When meeting someone for the first time
- "Good to see you again!" - When greeting someone you know
