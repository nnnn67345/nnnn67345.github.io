## The Lost Key

### Story

Sarah couldn't find her apartment key. She searched everywhere—in her bag, her pockets, on the floor. Nothing. She had left work late, and the building was now empty and dark.

Then she remembered. She had stopped at the coffee shop on the way home. Maybe she left it there?

When she arrived at the coffee shop, she found the owner sweeping the floor. "Excuse me," Sarah asked nervously. "Did you find a silver key?"

The owner's face lit up. "Yes! A customer brought it to the counter about an hour ago. You're lucky!"

Sarah felt relieved. "Thank you so much! You've saved my day."

The owner smiled. "No problem. Always happy to help."

As Sarah walked home with her key, she realized something important: kindness exists everywhere, even in small acts.

### Vocabulary

- **Lost** - Cannot find something
- **Remembered** - Brought back to mind
- **Relieved** - Felt better after worry
- **Kindness** - Quality of being nice and helpful

### Comprehension Questions

1. Where did Sarah lose her key?
2. What time did she go to the coffee shop?
3. Who found the key?
4. How did Sarah feel at the end?
